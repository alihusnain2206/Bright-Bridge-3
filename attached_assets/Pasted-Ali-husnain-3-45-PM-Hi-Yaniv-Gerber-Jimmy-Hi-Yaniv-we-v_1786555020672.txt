Yaniv Gerber  [3:49 PM]
In most cases, pass all locations, all employees, and all location -to-> employee relation in the dicts, for all sessions.

This would allow each employee to view their data across all locations, based on their permission levels. E.g. with LOCATION_READ  they could read others' data in locations when assigned to the same locations. With just regular SHIFT_READ for example, they can only access their own data. (edited) 
Yaniv Gerber  [3:50 PM]
If you have a more complex use case, e.g. different entities under the same organization, you can pass or not pass specific locations/employees in the dicts. For example, this is useful if you are trying to split an organization into sub-entities or franchises where some admins access all data and some admins access partial data.
But again, this is the more advanced use case. Most likely, passing all employee and all locations is the way to go.

Happy to go over it in detail, let me know! @Ali husnain
