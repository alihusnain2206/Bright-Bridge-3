Nir Tayeb  [1:41 PM]
Regarding the "wrong" clock in location - the web clock in location decided in the following order:

1. If the clock screen shows a location selector, that choice wins.
When the employer has enabled location selection on the time clock, whatever the employee picks in the dropdown is the location stamped on the shift.

2. If a selector is enabled but there's nothing to pick, the app pre-fills one.
It fills in, in this order:

the location of the shift the employee is currently scheduled for, if there is one;
their only location, if they're assigned to just one (assignment happens during token exchange with the location id);
their location set on the JWT


The employee sees this as fixed text rather than a dropdown, and it's what gets sent.

3. If location selection is turned off entirely, the shift falls back to the employee's location from the JWT - the one attached to their session when they signed in. (edited)
