=== PAGE 1: ALL EMPLOYEES (route /workforce/employees) ===
One row per employee for the selected period. Columns:
  Employee (name + job title) | Department | Hours Worked | Overtime |
  Punch Exceptions | Approval Status | Labor Cost
- Hours = payable hours from shifts in the period
- Overtime = hours above 40 per ISO week (same rule the Overview KPI uses)
- Punch Exceptions = count of missedPunch / extendedBreak / longShift
  flags for that employee, with a tooltip breaking them down
- Approval Status = Approved / Pending / Manager-edited (from our
  approvals data)
- Labor Cost = for HOURLY employees, hours × rate. For SALARIED
  employees, do NOT compute from hours — show "Salaried" or their
  period amount if available. ⚠️ Use the shared formatEmployeeRate /
  pay-type logic already in the codebase; salaried staff must never
  display an hourly rate or a $0 that looks like they aren't paid.
- Sortable columns, search by name, CSV export
- Clicking a row opens that employee's profile (/people/:id)
- Employees with zero hours still appear (with 0), but salaried staff
  must not be flagged as a problem for having no hours

=== PAGE 2: DEPARTMENTS (route /workforce/departments) ===
One row per department for the selected period. Columns:
  Department | Headcount | Total Hours | Overtime | Labor Cost |
  Avg Hours per Employee
- Expandable rows: clicking a department reveals its employees using the
  SAME shared table component from Page 1, filtered to that department
- Employees with no department assigned appear under "Unassigned" —
  do not hide them
- Sortable, CSV export
